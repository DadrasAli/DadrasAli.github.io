Software · FedBrew

FedBrew

FedBrew is an open-source research framework for controlled and reproducible federated learning experiments. Its modular architecture combines configuration validation, checks of specified software and scientific conditions, and self-describing run records to help identify deviations from the intended experimental protocol.

FedBrew is also designed for development by researchers and AI coding agents. Explicit interfaces, structured documentation, and automated checks guide the extension of algorithms, datasets, and models while helping identify compatibility issues and violations of stated scientific conditions.

Selected software
