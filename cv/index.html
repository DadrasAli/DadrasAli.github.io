Research profile

My research focuses on mathematical optimization and algorithm development for machine learning, particularly in federated and distributed settings. I establish theoretical guarantees for convergence and efficiency under data heterogeneity and communication constraints.

InterestsFederated and distributed learning, optimization algorithms, convergence analysis, personalization, and communication efficiency under heterogeneous data.
